Why TalentFlow

We Fix What's Broken in Recruitment

Legacy recruitment software loses candidate data, triggers silent overwrites, and relies on error-prone spreadsheets. Here is how TalentFlow fixes each bottleneck.

✕ Manual resume screening takes weeks

Algorithmic Match Scoring

Candidates are automatically scored (0-100%) against job specifications the moment their CV is submitted.

✕ Recruiters overwrite each other's card moves

Optimistic Concurrency Control

Database RowVersion tokens detect simultaneous moves and return HTTP 409 Conflict rather than losing data.

✕ Delayed candidate updates across disconnected tabs

Real-Time SignalR WebSockets

Every stage transition, interview booking, and candidate application broadcasts instantly across active team sessions.

✕ Candidate emails lost during server restarts

Transactional Outbox Pattern

Email events are saved atomically with business data. A background worker claims messages with UPDLOCK/READPAST.

✕ Manual, non-compliant Indian CTC calculations

Statutory CTC & Sanitized Offers

Automated breakdown of Basic, HRA, PF, and Gratuity with HTML-sanitized downloadable offer letters.

✕ Unauthorized access to confidential candidate reviews

Resource-Based IDOR Protection

Hiring managers can only access applications and notes for jobs explicitly assigned to their department.
